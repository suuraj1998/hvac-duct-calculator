Privacy Policy UPDATED OCTOBER 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the data collection and privacy practices of HVAC Duct Sizing & Airflow Calculator ("we", "our", or "the App"), accessible as an Android application and web portal at https://hvac-duct-calculator.web.app.

1. On-Device Local Data & Credentials Privacy

We prioritize user privacy. All HVAC duct calculations (CFM, L/s, fpm, m/s, Huebscher equivalent rectangular sizing, round snapping, friction loss rates, and velocity pressure) are performed 100% on your device.

  • Technician Credentials ("Trust This Device"): Any name, email address, or firm credentials saved in your local profile are stored exclusively in your device's local memory (SharedPreferences). They are never uploaded or transmitted to external servers.
  • Project Schedules: Multi-segment ductwork schedules and PDF job sheets remain on your local storage unless you choose to share them.
