HVAC Duct Sizing Suite - User Guide

Open the calculator

Quick start

  1. Open the Duct Sizer tab.
  2. Pick Rectangular or Round.
  3. Enter Airflow and Velocity (and Duct Height for rectangular ducts).
  4. The recommended duct size, friction loss, velocity pressure and free area update instantly.
  5. Tap Add to Schedule Table to save the result, or Export PDF Job Sheet to create a report.

Tab 1 - Duct Sizer

  • Rectangular / Round: choose the duct shape.
  • Airflow: air volume (CFM in Imperial, L/s in Metric).
  • Velocity: target air speed (fpm in Imperial, m/s in Metric).
  • Duct Height: the fixed height available; the width is calculated.
  • Live 2D cross-section: a drawing of the resulting duct.
  • Result card: recommended size, Huebscher circular equivalent, friction loss, velocity pressure, free area and the SMACNA / DW144 gauge suggestion.
  • Refresh icon: resets inputs to default values.

Tab 2 - Schedule Table

  • Lists every duct segment you added, with segment count and total airflow.
  • Segments are added from the Duct Sizer tab with Add to Schedule Table.
  • Export Schedule PDF creates a multi-segment report.

Tab 3 - Equal Friction

  • Quick reference for unit conversions: airflow, velocity, duct size, friction loss and velocity pressure.

Top bar buttons

  • Imperial / Metric: switch units. Values convert automatically.
  • Profile icon: save your name, email and company. Stored only on your device and used on PDFs.
  • Red reset icon: clears the app and starts from the beginning.
  • Question-mark icon: opens this guide inside the app.
  • Book icon: opens the Reference Library.

Reference Library

  • SMACNA Gauges: sheet-metal gauge, thickness, maximum duct dimension and reinforcement class.
  • DW144 (UK/EU): duct construction thickness and class data.
  • Velocity Limits: recommended air velocity ranges.

Exporting a PDF job sheet

  1. Tap Export PDF Job Sheet.
  2. A short sponsored ad appears first. Wait for the countdown, then tap Continue to PDF.
  3. Enter the job / system name, client location and technician name.
  4. Tap Generate PDF to download or share the report.

The ad helps keep this calculator free.

Tips and disclaimer

  • Typical comfort-duct velocities are 600-900 fpm for branches and up to about 1,500 fpm for mains.
  • Results are for guidance only. Verify against local codes and the engineer of record.
  • Your data stays on your device and is not sent to a server. See the privacy policy.
